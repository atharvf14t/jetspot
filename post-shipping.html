A quieter way to ship a company site

Nov 2, 2026 · Studio notes

Most company sites are loud because the brief was a list of pages, not a job. The visitor needs to understand the offer, trust the people, and take one step. Everything else is furniture.

Keep the proof close to the claim

A headline that says “we build systems” should be followed by a system someone is using, not a grid of icons. We put a short story, a number the client will stand behind, and a way to talk. Three beats. Then we stop.

Cut the page that exists to exist

On the last twelve builds we removed a careers page with no roles, a blog with two posts from 2022, and a services page that repeated the homepage in smaller type. The sites got faster to read, which is the only speed most visitors feel.

Motion that does not ask for attention

A marquee, a panel that opens, a card that slides. If a visitor can name the animation, it is probably in the way. We keep movement on transforms so the page stays smooth while someone scrolls past it.
