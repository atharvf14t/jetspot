What a useful CRM actually looks like

Oct 18, 2026 · Systems

A CRM fails when it asks the team to become data clerks. The useful version is the place a deal already lives, with as few fields as a person will fill in after a call.

One pipeline

If sales, delivery, and finance each keep a board, nobody trusts any of them. We start with the stages a job really passes through, name them in the team’s words, and refuse a second pipeline until the first one is dull and reliable.

Write down the handoff

The moment a lead becomes a client is where notes go missing. We put that handoff on one screen: what was promised, who owns delivery, and the date the work starts. The rest of the record can wait.

Integrate the inbox, not the universe

Email in, invoice out. Those two connections remove most of the copying. Everything else is a later project, and it should stay that way until someone feels the pain twice.
